# RDNA 2 (GFX10.3) resource descriptors

From Mesa 25.0.0 `src/amd/registers/gfx10-rsrc.json` (MIT). keylime writes these
into descriptor heap memory; the shader translator decodes the same words.

## Buffer (V#, 16 bytes)
- W0: BASE_ADDRESS[31:0]
- W1: BASE_ADDRESS_HI[0:15] STRIDE[16:29] CACHE_SWIZZLE[30] SWIZZLE_ENABLE[31]
- W2: NUM_RECORDS (bytes when stride 0, else elements)
- W3: DST_SEL_X[0:2] Y[3:5] Z[6:8] W[9:11] FORMAT[12:18] INDEX_STRIDE[21:22]
      ADD_TID_ENABLE[23] RESOURCE_LEVEL[24] LLC_NOALLOC[26:27] OOB_SELECT[28:29] TYPE[30:31]

## Image (T#, 32 bytes)
- W0: BASE_ADDRESS (address >> 8)[31:0]
- W1: BASE_ADDRESS_HI[0:7] MIN_LOD[8:19] FORMAT[20:28] WIDTH_LO[30:31]
- W2: WIDTH_HI[0:11] HEIGHT[14:27] RESOURCE_LEVEL[31]   (width-1, height-1)
- W3: DST_SEL_X..W[0:11] BASE_LEVEL[12:15] LAST_LEVEL[16:19] SW_MODE[20:24] BC_SWIZZLE[25:27] TYPE[28:31]
- W4: DEPTH[0:12] PITCH_MSB[13] BASE_ARRAY[16:28]
- W5: ARRAY_PITCH[0:3] MAX_MIP[4:7] MIN_LOD_WARN[8:19] PERF_MOD[20:22] CORNER_SAMPLES[23]
      LOD_HDW_CNT_EN[25] PRT_DEFAULT[26] BIG_PAGE[31]
- W6: COUNTER_BANK_ID[0:7] LLC_NOALLOC[8:9] ITERATE_256[10] MAX_UNCOMP_BLK[15:16] MAX_COMP_BLK[17:18]
      META_PIPE_ALIGNED[19] WRITE_COMPRESS_EN[20] COMPRESSION_EN[21] ALPHA_IS_ON_MSB[22]
      COLOR_TRANSFORM[23] META_DATA_ADDRESS_LO[24:31]
- W7: META_DATA_ADDRESS_HI

TYPE: 8 1D, 9 2D, 10 3D, 11 cube, 12 1D array, 13 2D array, 14 2D MSAA, 15 2D MSAA array.
DST_SEL: 0 zero, 1 one, 4 X, 5 Y, 6 Z, 7 W.

## Sampler (S#, 16 bytes)
- W0: CLAMP_X[0:2] Y[3:5] Z[6:8] MAX_ANISO_RATIO[9:11] DEPTH_COMPARE_FUNC[12:14]
      FORCE_UNNORMALIZED[15] ANISO_THRESHOLD[16:18] MC_COORD_TRUNC[19] FORCE_DEGAMMA[20]
      ANISO_BIAS[21:26] TRUNC_COORD[27] DISABLE_CUBE_WRAP[28] FILTER_MODE[29:30] SKIP_DEGAMMA[31]
- W1: MIN_LOD[0:11] MAX_LOD[12:23] (4.8 fixed) PERF_MIP[24:27] PERF_Z[28:31]
- W2: LOD_BIAS[0:13] LOD_BIAS_SEC[14:19] XY_MAG_FILTER[20:21] XY_MIN_FILTER[22:23] Z_FILTER[24:25]
      MIP_FILTER[26:27] MIP_POINT_PRECLAMP[28] ANISO_OVERRIDE[29] BLEND_ZERO_PRT[30]
- W3: BORDER_COLOR_PTR[0:11] BORDER_COLOR_TYPE[30:31]

## keylime notes
- Image FORMAT: keylime writes the GFX10 IMG_FORMAT when it knows it; until the
  translator lands it may hold the DXGI format (marked in descriptor.cpp).
